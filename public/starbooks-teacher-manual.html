Teacher Manual

A quick guide for using STARBOOKS in your classroom

STARBOOKS (Science and Technology Academic- and Research-Based Openly-Operated KioskS) is a free digital library that gives teachers and students access to science, technology, and education resources — online at starbooks.ph or offline through a school kiosk.

1. What is STARBOOKS?

  • Free digital library from DOST-STII launched in 2011.
  • Contains hundreds of thousands of S&T resources in text, audio, and video formats.
  • Includes research papers, journals, K–12 materials, videos, and reference volumes.
  • Works without internet through the offline stand-alone kiosk.
  • Complements the teacher — it does not replace classroom instruction.

2. How to Access STARBOOKS

  • Offline kiosk: Turn on the STARBOOKS unit at school and wait for the home screen.
  • Online: Visit www.starbooks.ph and register to access resources for free.
  • LAN (if available): Multiple users at school may access STARBOOKS through the local network.

3. Main Menus

  • Library — browse all available materials.
  • Search — find topics using keywords.
  • Categories — explore by subject, format, or grade level.
  • Videos — watch educational and Tamang DOSTkarte content.

4. How to Search a Topic

  • Click Search on the home screen.
  • Type your topic (e.g. "Climate Change" or "Solar Energy").
  • Review the list of articles, videos, and documents.
  • Open one resource and preview it before class.
  • Use it during discussion or assign it to students.

5. How to Browse by Category

  • Open Categories from the home screen.
  • Choose a subject such as Science, Technology, or K–12.
  • Select a resource that matches your lesson objective.
  • Plan how you will present or assign it to students.

6. When to Use STARBOOKS

  • Before class: Search and prepare supplementary materials.
  • During class: Show videos or articles; ask guide questions.
  • After class: Assign readings, research, or review tasks.

7. Sample Classroom Activity

  • Introduce the topic (e.g. Renewable Energy).
  • Open STARBOOKS and search for "Solar Energy."
  • Present the selected article or video to the class.
  • Discuss key points and ask: What is renewable energy? How does solar energy work?
  • Assign a follow-up activity or homework based on the resource.

8. Teacher Tips

  • Always preview resources before using them in class.
  • Combine STARBOOKS content with your own explanation and activities.
  • Use DOST-sourced materials as credible references for student research.
  • Encourage students to explore related topics independently.
  • Ask your school ICT coordinator if you need help with the kiosk.
Remember STARBOOKS provides information, references, videos, research materials, and activity ideas. You remain the guide — STARBOOKS is your extra assistant in bringing science and technology closer to learners.
